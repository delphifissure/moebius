# Pass 5 — Structured background models and background completion (agent report, lightly trimmed)

Sourcing note from the pass: arXiv/CVF/project pages were mostly egress-blocked; verification via snippets, GitHub repos and config files. Mechanisms stated from memory rather than fetched text are tagged [mem].

## 1. Room layout estimation
- Panorama lineage: LayoutNet (Zou et al., CVPR 2018); HorizonNet (Sun, Hsiao, Sun & Chen, CVPR 2019) — per-column floor–wall and ceiling–wall boundary rows + corners, bi-RNN, Manhattan pre-rotation; LED²-Net (Wang, Yeh, Sun, Chiu & Tsai, CVPR 2021 Oral, arXiv 2104.00568) — layout as depth along the horizon line with differentiable depth rendering, i.e. the layout IS a background depth map; LGT-Net (2203.01824), U²RLE (2304.08580), PanoTPS-Net (2510.11992, non-cuboid). Indoor, single room, tens of ms; walls snapped through doorways, clutter absorbed. Verdict: wrong modality (pano) but LED²-Net's "layout = renderable depth" is the structured background we lack.
- Perspective lineage: Hedau, Hoiem & Forsyth (ICCV 2009) parametric box + clutter; RoomNet (Lee et al., ICCV 2017, 1703.06241) keypoints for 11 cuboid types; Yang et al. non-cuboid layout (2104.07986: one floor, one ceiling, N vertical wall planes); uLayout (Solarte et al., 2025, 2503.21562) unifies perspective and pano via an equirectangular canvas and a HorizonNet-style per-column head; Total3DUnderstanding (Nie et al., CVPR 2020 Oral) layout box + camera + object boxes + meshes. Training data: Structured3D (Zheng et al., ECCV 2020) ships perspective layout annotations. Artefacts: hallucinated walls in corridors/open plans; fail outdoors; Total3D coarse. Verdict: a per-column floor-boundary row is the cheapest usable output — floor extent behind every object without depth; offline once per image, ~W numbers.

## 2. Planar segmentation as background continuation
- PlaneRCNN (Liu et al., CVPR 2019, 1812.04072); PlaneAE (Yu et al., CVPR 2019, 30 fps); PlaneTR (Tan et al., ICCV 2021, 2107.13108); PlaneRecTR (Shi, Zhi & Xu, ICCV 2023) and ++; PlaneSAM (2410.16545); MonoPlane (2411.01226 — planes fitted to monocular depth + normals with GC-RANSAC, generalises beyond ScanNet); AirPlanes (Watson et al., 2024); PLANA3R (2510.18714, zero-shot metric planes); Planar Gaussian Splatting (2412.01931). Learned nets collapse outdoors; over-segment textured walls.
- A plane (n, d) is defined for every pixel, so the occluded region behind an object in front of it is the plane evaluated there: the analytic replacement for a sagging membrane (a plane has no free interior). Verdict (high): adopt the MonoPlane recipe — fit a few large planes (RANSAC + normal consistency) to our own depth in the bake; zero real-time cost.

## 3. Horizon, gravity, ground plane
- Workman et al., Horizon Lines in the Wild (BMVC 2016); Zhai et al. (CVPR 2016) global-context horizon before VP search; Hold-Geoffroy et al. (CVPR 2018, 1712.01259) roll/pitch/FoV; UprightNet (Xian et al., ICCV 2019, 1908.07070); Perspective Fields (Jin et al., CVPR 2023 Highlight) per-pixel up-vector + latitude, indoor/outdoor; GeoCalib (Veicht, Sarlin, Lindenberger & Pollefeys, ECCV 2024) intrinsics + gravity via learned geometric optimisation; GroundNet (Man et al., ACM MM 2019, 1811.07222) ground plane by differentiable RANSAC on depth+normals gated by ground segmentation.
- Use: with gravity g and focal f, the ground plane has normal g through the lowest reliable floor samples — one scalar (camera height). Every pixel below the horizon then has a lower bound on background depth (no farther than the ground plane along its ray); the floor's own depth equals the bound, so it can never be classified as foreground. Verdict (high): GeoCalib/Perspective Fields offline once; gravity + horizon are the two "constants" the pipeline is missing, and both are physical.

## 4. Sky
- Liba et al., Sky Optimization (CVPRW 2020, 2006.10172): low-res sky net + guided-filter upsampling, < 0.5 s on phone; Castle in the Sky (Zou 2020, 2010.11800) video. Depth side: PrimeDepth (2409.09144) notes Marigold and it struggle with sky at infinity; "Modeling Depth Ambiguity" (arXiv 2606.02552, 2026, authors unverified) adds an explicit sky mixture component for threshold-free sky segmentation and flying-point-free skylines. Verdict (high): sky as a hard class, depth = far plane, never a rim/anchor for the membrane; in an 8-bit map sky is clamped at 255 anyway — the segmentation says which 255s are sky.

## 5. Scene de-occlusion, layered decomposition, background completion
- Zhan et al., Self-Supervised Scene De-occlusion (CVPR 2020, 2004.02788): PCNet-M amodal masks + pairwise order, PCNet-C fills occluded object content; completes objects, not the background; needs instance masks; offline.
- Dhamo et al., Peeking Behind Objects (1807.08776) and Object-Driven Multi-Layer Scene Decomposition (ICCV 2019, 1908.09521): LDI with a background RGB-D layer with objects removed [mem], synthetic-indoor training. Tulsiani, Tucker & Snavely, Layer-structured 3D Scene Inference (ECCV 2018, 1807.10264). Issaranon et al., Counterfactual Depth (1909.00915): depth with object X removed. Verdict: the honest precedent for hidden-background depth; indoor-synthetic, not robust in the wild, offline; the representation is what mesh + plug already emulates.
- Amodal Depth Anything (Li et al., ICCV 2025): RGB + amodal mask → relative depth of the occluded object part; ADIW compositing dataset (paste object, keep original depth as GT) — the zero-label trick for training a background-depth completer.
- pix2gestalt (Ozguroglu et al., CVPR 2024) whole-object synthesis; ObjectDrop (Winter et al., ECCV 2024, 2403.18818) counterfactual removal incl. shadows/reflections — colour only.
- Omnimatte (Lu et al., CVPR 2021) → Generative Omnimatte (Lee et al., 2025, 2411.16683); LayerDecomp; RevealLayer (2605.11818, 2026). Video or colour only; takeaway: background = what remains behind after removing all "things", i.e. by semantics.
- Gen3DSR (Ardelean, Özer & Egger, 3DV 2025): panoptic stuff vs things; things get amodal completion + per-object reconstruction; "the background is modeled as the surface that approximates the stuff entities collectively"; ≥ 20 GB VRAM. LayerPano3D (2408.13252), Scene4U (2504.00387): peel panoramas into occlusion layers, inpaint, refine as 3DGS. Verdict: stuff/things is the decision rule the 2024–25 literature converged on.

## 6. Depth-conditioned diffusion inpainting — what it gives for hidden depth
- ControlNet-depth (Zhang et al., ICCV 2023) and SD2-depth consume depth, do not produce it: texture stage only.
- SPIn-NeRF (Mirzaei et al., CVPR 2023): inpaints rendered disparity with LaMa as if an image; hidden depth = smooth continuation of the rim.
- NeRFiller (Weber et al., CVPR 2024): 2×2 grid-tiling prior; depth emerges from NeRF fitting.
- InFusion (Liu, Ouyang et al., 2024): Marigold-derived depth-completion diffusion model (inpainted RGB, incomplete depth, mask) → completed depth → Gaussians; ~20 steps + 150 fine-tune iterations. Cleanest "hidden background depth from one view" primitive.
- Text2Room (Höllein et al., ICCV 2023): SD2 inpainting + IronDepth as depth inpainting aligned to known depth; faces filtered by stretch/normal thresholds. Invisible Stitch (Engstler, Vedaldi, Laina & Rupprecht, 2024): a network fed (new image, existing geometry) → fused depth; trained NYUv2 + Places365 with Marigold pseudo-labels.
- SplatFill (2509.07809), Inpaint360GS (2511.06457), JointDiT (2505.00482, joint RGB-D generation).
- Common artefacts: hidden depth is a smooth interpolant of the rim, floors bulge into the hole, seams at silhouettes; seconds to minutes. Verdict: bake stage — depth completion conditioned on inpainted colour beats geometry-only extrapolation because colour says whether the hole is floor or wall.

## 7. Classical 3D-photo depth extrapolation
- Shih, Su, Kopf & Huang (CVPR 2020): verified from argument.yml/DOCUMENTATION.md — disparity difference > 0.04 after 5 passes of bilateral median (σs 4, σr 0.5, windows 7/7/5/5/5); edge fragments < 12 px dropped; dilation 10 px then 5 px; synthesis region 70 px, context region 140 px, border extrapolation 60 px; log-depth; 2–3 min per image. Mechanism [mem]: the LDI is cut along the edge; context grown only on the background side; synthesis grown from the background side into the foreground's former space; nets in order edge → colour → depth so a wall–floor crease continues behind the object.
- Kopf et al., One Shot 3D Photography (SIGGRAPH 2020, 2008.12298): discontinuities by disparity ratio; background layers extended by iteratively growing background pixels into the occluded region with smoothly continued background depth, iteration count bounds parallax reach [mem]; seconds on a phone.
- SLIDE (ICCV 2021): two soft layers, background depth + colour inpainted in one pass. AdaMPI (SIGGRAPH 2022), SinMPI (SIGGRAPH Asia 2023): EdgeConnect-style edge → depth → image background inpainter.
- Verdict: the unanimous rule is extrapolate depth from the far side only, edges first; we lack the edge-first structural continuation and a bound on how far extrapolated depth may run.

## What we should steal (prioritised by the pass)
1. Gravity + horizon + focal from GeoCalib (or Perspective Fields), once per image; ground plane by fitting camera height to depth below the horizon with normals ≈ g.
2. Stuff/things panoptic split as the background-membership rule: only things may tear; stuff is background even without holes.
3. Planes fitted to our own depth on stuff pixels (MonoPlane recipe); background depth behind a thing = nearest supporting plane along the ray, clamped by the ground plane; membrane only where no plane exists.
4. Shih's edge-first ordering and far-side-only context (0.04 disparity, 70/140 px, log-depth as defensible starting constants).
5. Sky as a hard class, excluded from every rim/anchor set.
6. Bake: InFusion / Invisible-Stitch-style depth completion conditioned on inpainted colour for residual holes; ControlNet-depth for texture.
7. ADIW compositing trick for free training data if 6 is too slow.

## Recommended background model
Hybrid: planes where a surface can be named, membrane only in the remainder, membership by semantics first and geometry second. Pixel rule in order: (i) sky → background at infinity; (ii) stuff class → background regardless of holes; (iii) thing whose depth ≤ supporting-plane depth − ε along its ray → foreground layer; (iv) else background. ε = the depth quantum scaled by the blur radius. Background depth behind a thing: nearest stuff plane along the ray (floor first, else wall), bounded by the ground plane; membrane anchored to far rims only where no plane, never to sky or things. Real-time cost: a few dot products per vertex; networks run once in the bake.
