# Pass 2 — Single-image 3D photography and layered scene representations (agent report, lightly trimmed)

Sourcing note from the pass: arXiv/CVF mostly blocked; GitHub reachable. Read in full: SLIDE (paper + supplement), Tucker & Snavely, Worldsheet, Kopf's supplement. Code level: Shih, Niklaus, SynSin, TMPI, AdaMPI, MINE, single-view MPI, Flash3D config. Abstract/README level: the rest. Constants quoted from code or paper text; memory-derived items flagged (unverified).

## 1. Layered Depth Images — Shade, Gortler, He & Szeliski, SIGGRAPH 1998
Per lattice position zero-to-many (colour, depth) samples; McMillan epipolar ordering so alpha compositing needs no z-sort; splatting; built from multiple views/ray tracing, no hallucination. Lesson: hidden content stored per lattice position, so plug depth/colour varies per pixel. Bonus (verified): Zitnick et al., SIGGRAPH 2004 — main layer + thin boundary layer around discontinuities with Bayesian-matted colour/depth/alpha, matte dilated 1 px inward.

## 2. Shih, Su, Kopf & Huang, CVPR 2020 (code-level)
LDI with explicit 4-connectivity; cut at depth edges; per edge, learned inpainting edge → depth → colour in a local patch; merged as new LDI samples; exported as a mesh. Depth prep (utils.read_MiDaS_depth, bilateral_filtering.py): MiDaS disparity min-subtracted, 3×3 box blur, rescaled to [0, 3], depth = 1/max(disp, 0.05); sparse bilateral filtering 5 iterations, windows [7,7,5,5,5], σ_s 4 px, σ_r 0.5, range kernel zeroed across discontinuities. Edges: 4-neighbour |Δ(1/depth)| > 0.04 (~1.3 % of range); tear_edges records near/far partners; components cleaned (remove_dangling, remove_redundant_edge, redundant_number 12, depth_edge_dilate 10 then 5). Context/synthesis (context_and_holes): flood outward on the far side to background_thickness 70 px (synthesis) and context_thickness 140 px at a 960-px long side (~7 % / 15 %); second pass 70/70; extrapolation_thickness 60 px; patches resized to 512 for EdgeConnect edge, depth, partial-conv colour nets. Limits: hard layering loses thin/hairy structures; slow; tiny trajectories (x 0.015, z 0.05 normalised) so fill depth never tested near 45°. Verdict: steal the depth pre-processing; fill extent should come from the known eye offset, not 70 px.

## 3. Kopf et al., One Shot 3D Photography, SIGGRAPH 2020
Mobile depth net (Tiefenrausch, 384×288, MegaDepth) → LDI → inpainting on the LDI (Farbrausch) → chart atlas + mesh. Discontinuities by disparity thresholds linked into depth edges; "not limited to at most two layers"; constraints shape how a depth edge CONTINUES into the disoccluded region; inpainting net trained in 2D, run on LDI pixels by connectivity traversal; LDI partitioned into flat charts by scanline seed-and-grow, atlas-packed, meshed, simplified; a few seconds on a phone. Thresholds unobtainable (unverified). Verdict: closest production analogue; two ideas — continue the depth edge into the hole so the plug's depth profile is shaped, not flat; a multi-layer LDI represents interior self-occlusion naturally.

## 4. Niklaus, Mai, Yang & Liu, 3D Ken Burns, SIGGRAPH Asia 2019 (code-level)
Depth → semantic adjustment → boundary refinement → point cloud → render from extreme view → joint RGB-D inpainting → append points. Semantic adjustment: Mask R-CNN instances (score ≥ 0.7, person/vehicle/animal, masks ≥ 64 px) get disparity replaced by the max disparity of the bottom 3 % rows (after two 3×3 min-pools) — anchors objects to their ground contact. Refinement U-Net (image + disparity) sharpens boundaries. Edges/skirts: points DROPPED where |Laplacian(normalised disparity)| ≥ 0.03 (tenValid) — no skirts. Disocclusion: render from 1.1× the maximum shift; mask = pixels with nothing rendered (median-5); net outputs RGB and disparity; only new pixels appended. CUDA fill_disocclusion scans 16 directions and fills from the endpoint with GREATER depth — the far rim. Constants: f = 512 px, baseline 40. Verdict: the validity mask and far-side fill are directly transferable and cheap.

## 5. Jampani et al., SLIDE, ICCV 2021 Oral (read in full)
Two layers: FG = RGB + disparity D + soft visibility A; BG = inpainted RGB-D; both triangle meshes; composite A·I + (1 − A)·Ĩ; 0.07 s (0.35 s with matting). Depth prep: MiDaS v2 normalised to [0,1], slight Gaussian blur and MAX-POOL (dilates the foreground so mixed boundary pixels fall on the FG layer). Soft visibility (Eq. 1): A = exp(−β‖∇D‖²), Sobel gradient — the cliff becomes transparent instead of torn. Soft disocclusion map (Eqs. 2–3): pixel can be disoccluded if ∃ neighbour with D(x,y) − D(x_i,y_j) > ρ·K, K = pixel distance; softened S = ReLU(tanh(γ·max(·))), along horizontal/vertical scanlines (m > 30 too costly), optionally low-res; swapping > for < gives the occlusion map. β, ρ, γ, m NOT stated in paper or supplement (checked); a public re-implementation uses β = 3. Matting (§3.3): U2Net saliency → FBA matting → alpha M; A' = A·(1 − (M̄ − M)(1 − Ŝ)), M̄ = max-pooled M — visibility lowered only in the thin band around the matte. Depth-aware inpainting (§3.4): DeepFillv2 gated-conv, RGB-D output, Places2, 256² crops, trained with OCCLUSION masks (fattened FG; GT the real image) + random strokes — teaches borrowing only from farther pixels. Limits: fails when depth or matting fails; two layers ⇒ no interior self-occlusion; RE10K t = 10 baselines. Verdict: the single most reusable paper — geometry-derived disocclusion extent, soft alpha instead of tearing, matting only in a rim band.

## 6. Single-view MPIs
- Tucker & Snavely, CVPR 2020 (read; code): 32 RGBA planes, disparity-linear depths 1–100; colour per plane blends the input image and ONE predicted global background image with weight w_i = Π_{j>i}(1 − α_j); harmonic alpha bias 1/i; edge-aware smoothness e_min 0.1, g_min 0.05; scale-invariant loss on RE10K. Without the background image: "blurriness and repeated edge artefacts at depth boundaries". Verdict: validates a smooth membrane — the learned background is low-frequency too.
- MINE, ICCV 2021: continuous-depth MPI/NeRF hybrid, 32/64 planes; per-image network inference.
- AdaMPI, SIGGRAPH 2022: plane-depth adjustment + depth-aware colour nets; 64 planes at 256×384 with DPT disparity; warp-back training (EdgeConnect inpainting); authors filter disparity around discontinuities and clean the warp mask; inpainting fails under large viewpoint change.
- TMPI, ICCV 2023 (config.py, tmpi.py): square tiles 0.125 × width clamped [64, 256] px, 12.5 % overlap, ≤ 1024 px; per tile, disparity normalised and CONFIDENCE-WEIGHTED K-MEANS WITH 3 CLUSTERS places 4 planes; RGB prior per plane by pyramid hole-filling from the tile's own pixels; a net refines RGBA; OpenGL renderer; flicker at some tile edges. Verdict: local clustering is a principled tuning-free way to pick plug depth (farthest local mode), agnostic to object vs wall.
- SinMPI, SIGGRAPH Asia 2023: SD outpainting beyond the frustum + fine-tuned depth-aware inpainter; per-scene, slow.
- MPI edge behaviour: soft alpha spreads a cliff over 2–3 planes (fewer stretch artefacts, ghost/echo edges at large parallax); all trained at ≤ RE10K baselines.

## 7. Worldsheet (ICCV 2021) and SynSin (CVPR 2020)
Worldsheet: 33×33 (or 65×65) grid deformed by predicted offsets + depth at 256²; inpainting net refines; explicit limitation: a single sheet connects foreground to background, "artifacts near object boundaries"; their fix is multiple alpha-textured sheets (= tear + plug). SynSin: feature point cloud splatted with radius 4 px, K = 128 points per pixel, τ = 1, weighted-sum accumulation; refinement GAN. Neither yields a real-time asset without the network.

## 8. Generative single-image NVS, 2023–2026
ZeroNVS (CVPR 2024; ~3 h/scene SDS); GenWarp (NeurIPS 2024; warped-coordinate embedding decides where to warp vs generate; 3–4 views → 3DGS in ~30 s); ViewCrafter (2024; DUSt3R cloud conditions video diffusion, ~120 s/A100); Stable Virtual Camera (ICCV 2025; 1.3B; v1.0 had "foreground objects detached from the background"); WonderJourney (CVPR 2024; boundary-depth, sky-depth and disocclusion-aware refinement; scale-ambiguity distortion); Invisible Stitch (2024; depth completion conditioned on image + partial depth + mask; NYUv2 + Places365); LucidDreamer (TVCG 2025); Flash3D (3DV 2025; feed-forward, 2 Gaussians per pixel, the second parametrised as a POSITIVE DEPTH INCREMENT behind the first; UniDepth backbone) — the only fast method that literally learns "a layer behind"; Splatt3R (2024) and DepthSplat (CVPR 2025) need ≥ 2 views, Splatt3R does not hallucinate. Verdict: none produces a per-pixel layered asset for the browser in real time; offline value: a diffusion inpainter for plug colour with depth-aware masks (SLIDE), depth-completion for plug depth (Invisible Stitch).

## What we should steal (prioritised by the pass)
1. Geometry-derived fill extent (SLIDE Eqs. 2–3): with the eye offset bounded (≈ tan 45° × portal distance), the disocclusion condition D(x,y) − D(x_i,y_j) > K/(g·b_max) — g the parallax gain (px per disparity unit), b_max the max offset — has no free constant; plug depth need never exceed the depth that satisfies it, bounding both "too deep" and "tunnelling".
2. Soft visibility instead of a hard tear (SLIDE Eq. 1): α = exp(−β‖∇D‖²) on the front mesh; set β so the smallest disparity step already treated as a cliff spread over the blur width gives α ≈ 0; removes skirts and lets porous silhouettes show the plug through them.
3. Drop cliff vertices by Laplacian (Niklaus, 0.03 on normalised disparity) as a second cheap skirt filter, robust to 8-bit quantisation if applied after blurring.
4. Fill from the far rim only (Niklaus fill_disocclusion; SLIDE occlusion-mask training): formalise "never sample colour or depth from pixels nearer than the rim"; train/prompt any learned inpainter with fattened-foreground masks.
5. Edge-aware sparse bilateral sharpening of disparity (Shih) before tearing; then max-pool by the blur radius (SLIDE) so mixed pixels land on the front layer.
6. Local depth clustering for plug depth (TMPI: 3 confidence-weighted k-means modes per 12.5 %-width tile): farthest local mode = plug depth; no object/wall segmentation needed.
7. Rim-band matting for foliage/hair (SLIDE §3.3), offline per image with fixed models.
8. Multiple LDI samples per pixel (Shade, Kopf): interior self-occlusion needs a second plug sample behind the first; Flash3D's positive depth increment is the constraint.

## Open problems the field still has
No single-image method recovers hidden-surface depth (backs, concavities); every 3D-photo pipeline is designed and evaluated at RE10K-scale baselines — 45° off-axis is outside all published regimes; porous/semi-transparent silhouettes only via mattes, which fail where depth fails; depth-edge vs colour-edge misalignment and quantised depth remain heuristics with hand thresholds; generative NVS produces frames, not per-pixel layered assets; scale/parallax ambiguity of relative depth is unsolved — every method fixes one arbitrary gain.
