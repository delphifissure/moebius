# S17 — the SD hand-off audited and repaired (2026-09-11)

The user's instruction: "double check C — both the visualization of the SD gaps and the atlas… make sure the artist can
see live what will be inpainted, and make sure the atlas is correct". Two things hand the plane bake to the diffusion
stage: the live **SD regions** view (`sdRegionsChk`) and the **SD Bundle** export (`exportSDBundle`). Both predate the
plane arm. This note records what they did before, what was changed, and how the change was checked. Line numbers
are the app's file after the change unless marked *(before)*. Scripts: `research/s17/`. Frames and dumps:
`moebiusv2/harness/shots/c_audit/{before,after}_{photo_da3,photo_8bit,S15}` (not committed; regenerated by
`harness/c_audit.js`).

## 1. What was found (read-only audit, before any change)

**The live view.** `sdHighlightLogicGLSL` tinted a background fragment cyan where `u_sdMask` — the band `disocc`, after
`_bandReplace` — was set, orange beyond the frame; uncovered pixels took the A210 backdrop. But:

1. **The tint was the band, and the wash is not painted on the band.** The plane colour is synthesised on the
   *carriers with a far side* (`carQ3[i] && plateQ[i] < dQ[i] − q`, the `hasC` set), and band texels that are their own
   far side keep the source colour. Measured on the three audit bakes (band texels that keep the source, i.e. tinted for
   nothing; carrier-only texels that carry a wash, i.e. placeholders shown untinted):

   | bake | band | carriers | synthesised (hasC) | band ∖ synthesised (over-claimed) | synthesised ∖ band (missed) |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   | photograph, DA3-Mono-Large | 265 899 | 354 181 | 347 225 | 6 956 | 88 282 |
   | photograph, old 8-bit map | 412 329 | 476 941 | 469 849 | 7 088 | 64 608 |
   | S15 (sky, plate 2) | 48 020 | 50 354 | 46 882 | 3 472 | 2 334 |

2. **Plate 2 was never marked.** Its material is `matQ.clone()`; the checkbox handler set `bgLayerMesh.material` only,
   and plate 2 sampled plate 1's band mask — yet every plate-2 texel is a synthesised colour (76 998 on DA3, 58 666 on
   the 8-bit map, 19 523 on S15). The step faces (a rim-mean colour over their whole area) likewise.
3. **The sky layer had no highlight at all** (`MeshBasicMaterial`), although below the source sky each column is the
   continued horizon colour and beyond the frame it is the clamp-extended margin. On S15 at fx 0.6 the sky's
   beyond-frame margin rendered as plain sky while the backdrop next to it was orange (sheet `s17_S15_sheet.png`,
   "before" column).
4. The band tier (the part the texture stage paints when a tier is set) was not distinguished from the outer band that
   keeps the wash.

**The bundle.** After a plane bake, `exportSDBundle` wrote 8 files, all at the canvas size (572×322 in the harness), all
8-bit: the screen-space contract of the current pose (`color`, `depth_completed`, `mask_inpaint`, `mask_outpaint`,
`mask_fg_occluder`) and the legacy `src_band_mask` / `src_bg_depth_completed` / `src_bg_color_baked` from the pass-1
fronts build — **not the plane bake on screen**. The native-resolution `dir_*` and `out_*` sets were skipped because
`bgDirectionalExport` and `bgExtendExport` are assigned in `buildBackgroundLayer`'s v1 branch, after the quick bake's
return (`bake.json`: `dirExport:false, extExport:false` on all three bakes). Plate 2, the source depth the bake used,
the carriers, the placeholder set, the sky, the margin and every convention (depth law, quantum, envelope) were absent.
A ControlNet fed `src_bg_depth_completed.png` after a plane bake would have been conditioned on the wrong depth.

## 2. What was changed (app; the plate law untouched — §4)

**Placeholder classes.** In the plane-colour block, right after `hasC` is complete, a per-texel class
(`window._qbPlatePaint`, source rows): **1** synthesised colour inside the tier (or no tier set) — *paint*;
**2** synthesised, in the band but outside the tier — the wash may stay; **3** synthesised, carrier-only (never
demanded, coloured for continuity); **0** source colour. `hasC` is the truth of "synthesised"; the tier is
`_qbBandTier`. A float `DataTexture` of the class (`u_sdPaint`, flipped rows like `maskF`) is bound on plate 1 (and the
ring strips, which share its material); plate 2 gets its own (`has2` → class **4**); the step faces set `u_sdPaintAll`.
`u_sdMask` (the band) is untouched — it still gates the A84 stretch cut and the fragment tear.

**The view.** `sdHighlightLogicGLSL` tints by class: cyan 1, blue 2, teal 3, magenta 4, the white 2-texel rim edge as
before, orange beyond the frame; the FG dimmed ×0.35. The sky layer is now a small `ShaderMaterial` (raw texel
passthrough, as the plate's own shader) with `u_sdHighlight`, a `u_sdPaint` of its own (1 = not source sky, i.e. the
continued horizon colour) and the frame test on its UV (beyond [0,1] → orange). The checkbox handler walks
`bgLayerMesh.userData.{plate2, steps, sky, back}`. A **check view** `u_sdPaintOnly` (`window._applySdPaintOnly(on)`,
harness only) renders every fragment white where its colour is a placeholder, black elsewhere, the FG black, the
backdrop white — so "the tint equals the placeholder set" is a pixel count (§3). The checkbox's tooltip carries the
legend; `meta.plane.liveView` repeats it.

**The bundle.** A `plane_*` set, gated on `bgFarRuleOn() && _bgQuickBaked`, built from the quick bake's own captures
(the arrays `harness/a257_probe.js` dumps): native resolution, rows top-first, depths as **16-bit** grey PNG written by
`_png16Gray` (IHDR bit depth 16 / colour type 0, IDAT = zlib of stored deflate blocks — RFC 1950/1951 §3.2.4 — with
Adler-32; CRC-32 shared with the zip writer, now module-level `_crc32`). Files:

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `plane_source_color.png`, `plane_source_depth16.png` | the picture at the plate grid; the source depth the bake used (dequantised, conditioned) |
| `plane_plate_depth16.png`, `plane_plate_color.png` | plate 1: the far field on the carriers, the source elsewhere; the wash on the placeholder set |
| `plane_mask_inpaint.png` | white = classes 1–3: **the inpaint region** |
| `plane_mask_inpaint_tier.png` | class 1 only (when a tier is set) |
| `plane_mask_class.png` | class × 60 |
| `plane_mask_band.png`, `plane_mask_carriers.png`, `plane_plate_torn.png` | the band, the carriers, the torn set |
| `plane_band_first_uncover.png` | first-uncover pose fraction (0–255), alpha 0 outside the band (the S6 file, now actually emitted) |
| `plane_plate2_depth16.png`, `plane_plate2_color.png`, `plane_plate2_mask.png` | plate 2 where it exists |
| `plane_sky_mask.png`, `plane_sky_color.png` | (sky on) source sky texels; the sky layer's texture |
| `plane_out_mask_outpaint.png`, `plane_out_color.png`, `plane_out_depth16.png` | (margin on) the plate clamp-extended by the A245 margin (Mx, My), as the strips render it |
| `meta.json` → `plane` | native size, plate options, depth convention with `outerVolumeDepth / innerVolumeDepth / currentNormPortalPlane`, source grid / σ / visible step / effective quantum / sky threshold, envelope (half-angles, aspect, D, terrarium), ground plane, counts (band, carriers, placeholders by class, plate 2, sky, torn, clones), the class legend, notes |

When the plane set is emitted the legacy `src_*` trio is **omitted** and `meta.legacy_omitted` says why; the
screen-space five stay, listed under `meta.poseDependent`. Without a plane bake the bundle is as before (the cold
export still runs without a bake: `harness/sdbundle.js`, no alert, screen-space files only). `_plugGeoBand` resets the
new captures (`_qbPlatePaint`, `_qbPlate2Has`, `_qbSkyColor`, `_qbMargin`) so a bundle can only carry this bake's.

## 3. Checks

`harness/c_audit.js` bakes exactly as the panel's Build does (selects → `_applyPlateOptions` → `_plugGeoBand({flush,
observed, gateAPriori})`; plane · wash · picture margin · tier 35° · stretched seams; sky on for S15), captures the
bundle instead of downloading it, renders three poses (rest; fx 0.6; fx 1.0 fy −0.5, fractions of the envelope) in the
plain, SD-regions and check views, and dumps the bake's arrays.

**Bundle = bake** (`research/s17/c_bundle_check.py`, every file against the same bake's arrays): all three bakes
**ALL OK** — depths within 0.5/65535 (rounding; values a hair below 0 are clamped: 19 texels on the photograph,
S15's 103 808 sky texels, which sit below zero under the plane at infinity and read 0 — `plane_sky_mask` names
them), colours and masks texel-identical, the class law holds (no class-3 texel in the band, no class-1/2 outside it,
placeholders ⊂ carriers), the margin files are the exact edge extension, `src_*` absent with the meta note. Sizes:
23 files / 24.7 MB (DA3), 23 / 24.3 MB (8-bit), 25 / 17.0 MB (S15).

## 4. What this does not do

- **No reimport of the plane set.** `importSDInpaintedPatch` builds a legacy patch mesh from one colour+depth pair; the
  per-plane v2 importer expects `v2_*` names. Putting an inpainted `plane_plate_color.png` (and plate 2, sky, margin)
  back onto the plate is the next hand-off gap; it was not in the instruction and was not built.
- The class-2 texels (band outside the tier, 47 845 on DA3) are exported as placeholders *and* flagged; whether the wash
  stays there is the artist's choice, which is why the tier file exists.
- The 0.19 % of white at rest on S15 (thin outlines) are plate slivers showing through the torn foreground at rest — a
  rendering fact of the torn FG, not of this change; recorded, not addressed.
